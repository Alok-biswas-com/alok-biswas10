- Aria-labels for navigation: `aria-label="Main Navigation"`

### ✅ ARIA Labels & Attributes
```html

  <a href="#hero" class="logo" title="Alok Biswas - Professional Graphic Designer - alokbiswas10">Alok Biswas</a>
```

